🔐 About Cryptography

Cryptography is the science of secure communication — protecting information from unauthorized access.

📖 What is Cryptography?

Cryptography is the practice and study of techniques for securing communication in the presence of third parties called adversaries. It involves creating written or generated codes that allow information to be kept secret.

Modern cryptography exists at the intersection of mathematics, computer science, electrical engineering, communication science, and physics.

🏛️ History

Cryptography has a long history dating back to ancient Egypt. Julius Caesar used a simple substitution cipher (the Caesar cipher) to send secret messages. During World War II, the Enigma machine was used by Germany to encrypt military communications.

The modern era began with public-key cryptography in the 1970s, revolutionizing how we secure digital communications.

⚙️ Types of Cryptography

🔒

Symmetric

Same key used for both encryption and decryption. Examples: AES, DES, 3DES.

🗝️

Asymmetric

Public and private key pair. Examples: RSA, ECC, Diffie-Hellman.

#️⃣

Hash Functions

One-way function that produces a fixed-size output. Examples: MD5, SHA-256, SHA-512.
